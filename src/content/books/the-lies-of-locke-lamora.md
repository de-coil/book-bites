## The Lies of Locke Lamora Review

### A Book About Lies (And More Lies)

The novel opens with a thief who is very good at his job. He leads a gang of gentlemen bastards who specialize in elaborate cons.

Locke Lamora is charming, clever, and always one step ahead. Until he isn't.

A mysterious Gray King is also running cons. This complicates things.

---

### Plot, Con Artist Style

The narrative follows Locke as he schemes:

- He leads his gang through elaborate cons
- He falls in love (complicating)
- He encounters the Gray King (dangerous)
- He makes mistakes (costly)
- He lies to everyone (including himself)

A con within a con within a con is attempted. It almost works.

---

### Characters, Mostly Dishonest

The cast is charmingly corrupt:

- **Locke Lamora** – Thief. Good at lying, bad at honesty
- **Jean Tannen** – Locke's best friend. Strong. Patient. Long-suffering
- **The Gentlemen Bastards** – A gang of thieves. Each has specialties
- **The Gray King** – Mysterious. Dangerous. Well-organized
- **Sabetha** – Clever. Dangerous. Locke's equal in many ways

Character development occurs through betrayal and reconciliation.

---

### Themes, Deceptively

The book explores:

- the art of the con as high art
- loyalty among thieves (sometimes)
- whether love is worth the risk
- the importance of a good lie

A con is described in detail. It is somehow still interesting.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "Trust is the most valuable currency, and I intend to counterfeit as much of it as possible."

Locke says this with a straight face. Almost.

---

### Final Thoughts

This is a book about lies, loyalty, and elaborate cons. It contains all three in equal measure.

Recommended for readers who enjoy:

- clever cons and elaborate schemes
- witty banter and wordplay
- characters who lie to everyone
- Venice-like settings with canals

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 perfectly forged documents**.
