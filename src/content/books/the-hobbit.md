## The Hobbit Review

### A Book About Reluctance (And Caves)

The novel opens with a hobbit who wants nothing more than a quiet life and regular meals. A wizard arrives and ruins everything.

Bilbo Baggins is recruited for an adventure he didn't ask for. He complains about it. He continues anyway.

A dragon is involved. The dragon has opinions about gold.

---

### Plot, As It Were

The narrative follows Bilbo as he travels:

- He leaves his comfortable home (reluctantly)
- He joins a group of dwarves (who sing constantly)
- He encounters trolls, goblins, and spiders
- He finds a ring (important later)
- He meets a dragon (very large)
- There is a battle (of five armies)

A riddle contest in a cave changes everything. Gollum is very upset about losing.

---

### Characters, Mostly Hairy Feet

The cast is varied:

- **Bilbo** – Hobbit. Good at burgling, bad at wanting to be on adventures
- **Gandalf** – Wizard. Arrives uninvited. Leaves mysteriously
- **Thorin** – Dwarf king. Very serious about reclaiming his homeland
- **Gollum** – Lives in a cave. Has relationship issues with a ring
- **Smaug** – Dragon. Large. Flammable. Has opinions about treasure

Character development occurs through unexpected courage and good timing.

---

### Themes, Naturally

The book explores:

- the value of home and comfort
- whether small people can do great things
- the corrupting influence of treasure
- the importance of good manners (even to dragons)

A description of a landscape lasts several pages. It is somehow still interesting.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "I am a burglar, or a sneak, or something of the sort."

Bilbo says this with increasing confidence throughout the book.

---

### Final Thoughts

This is a book about adventure, dragons, and reluctant heroes. It contains all three in equal measure.

Recommended for readers who enjoy:

- cozy beginnings leading to epic journeys
- riddles and puzzles
- dragons with personality
- characters who surprise themselves

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 perfectly comfortable armchairs**.
