## Before They Are Hanged Review

### A Book About Journeying (And Suffering)

The novel continues the First Law trilogy. The characters are sent on a mission. It goes poorly.

Jezal dan Luthar is still useless. Glokta is still torturing people. Logen Ninefingers is still becoming the Bloody-Nine.

The world is ending. Again.

---

### Plot, Southward Bound

The narrative follows multiple characters:

- Jezal and company travel south (unwillingly)
- Glokta investigates treason (painfully)
- Logen and the Northmen fight (constantly)
- Bayaz seeks something (important)
- Things go wrong (expected)

A journey south is described. It is very long.

---

### Characters, Still Grim

The cast continues:

- **Jezal** – Still useless. Still handsome. Very confused
- **Glokta** – Inquisitor. Tortures people. Very pragmatic
- **Logen** – Warrior. Becomes the Bloody-Nine. Very tired
- **Bayaz** – Wizard. Knows things. Very old
- **Ferro** – Warrior. Angry. Very determined

Character development occurs through suffering and survival.

---

### Themes, Grimly

The book explores:

- the cost of heroism
- whether the world can be saved
- the nature of power and corruption
- the importance of not being heroic

A journey south lasts several chapters. It is somehow still interesting.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "The hero has the courage to do what needs to be done. The wise man has the sense to know it's not worth it."

This is repeated until it becomes a warning.

---

### Final Thoughts

This is a book about journeys, suffering, and grimdark. It contains all three in equal measure.

Recommended for readers who enjoy:

- grimdark fantasy
- characters who suffer constantly
- journeys that go wrong
- anti-heroes who aren't heroic

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 perfectly grim journeys**.
