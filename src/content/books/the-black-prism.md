## The Black Prism Review

### A Book About Color (And Consequences)

The novel opens with a magic system based on colors. Each color has properties and costs. Drafting too much is fatal.

Gavin Guile is the Prism, the most powerful drafter. He has secrets.

Kip, his bastard son, doesn't know his father. This is about to change.

---

### Plot, Chromatically Speaking

The narrative follows Kip as he discovers his heritage:

- He learns he's Gavin's son
- He attends the Chromeria (magic school)
- He discovers he can draft all colors (unusual)
- Political intrigue unfolds (dangerous)
- War approaches (inevitable)

A Prism's power is explained. It involves light and color.

---

### Characters, Colorfully Drawn

The cast is varied:

- **Gavin Guile** – The Prism. Powerful. Has secrets (many)
- **Kip** – Bastard son. Can draft all colors. Very confused
- **Karris** – Black Guard. Loyal. Very efficient
- **Andross Guile** – Gavin's father. Manipulative. Very old
- **Fisk** – Kip's friend. Loyal. Not magical

Character development occurs through revelation and training.

---

### Themes, Spectrally

The book explores:

- the cost of power
- family secrets and their consequences
- whether destiny can be changed
- the importance of color balance

A magic system is explained in detail. It is somehow still interesting.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "A Prism can draft all colors. This is both a gift and a curse."

This is repeated until it becomes a warning.

---

### Final Thoughts

This is a book about color magic, family secrets, and political intrigue. It contains all three in equal measure.

Recommended for readers who enjoy:

- unique color-based magic systems
- political intrigue and secrets
- characters discovering their heritage
- the cost of magical power

Overall rating: **7 out of 10 perfectly balanced prisms**.
