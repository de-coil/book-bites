## Dune Review

### A Book About Sand (And Other Things)

The novel opens with a planet made entirely of sand, political intrigue, and a substance that makes space travel possible. The protagonist, Paul, is expected to be a duke but would probably prefer to be anywhere else.

His mother has been breeding people like livestock for generations. This is presented as normal.

The book contains more internal monologue than external dialogue. Characters think things at each other intensely.

---

### Plot, Insofar As There Is One

The narrative follows Paul as he navigates desert politics:

- His father dies (politically, then literally)
- He and his mother flee into the desert
- He learns to ride giant worms
- He becomes a religious figure against his will
- Everyone argues about the spice

A crysknife is introduced. It is sharp. It remains sharp throughout.

---

### Characters Of Note

The cast is politically minded:

- **Paul** – Reluctant messiah. Good at prescience, bad at avoiding destiny
- **Jessica** – Paul's mother. Has too many skills and not enough patience
- **Baron Harkonnen** – Floats. Schemes. Has opinions about grooming
- **Stilgar** – Leads the Fremen. Very serious about water discipline
- **Chani** – Knows things. Sometimes tells Paul things

Character development occurs through exposure to sand and prophecy.

---

### Themes, Possibly

The book explores:

- the dangers of charismatic leaders
- ecology as politics
- whether prescience is a gift or a curse
- the importance of moisture retention

A conversation about water rights lasts several chapters. It is somehow still interesting.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "Fear is the mind-killer."

This litany is repeated until fear itself becomes afraid of being killed.

---

### Final Thoughts

This is a book about politics, ecology, and giant worms. It contains all three in equal measure.

Recommended for readers who enjoy:

- complex political intrigue
- desert planets with too much sand
- religious fervor as a plot device
- characters who think very loudly

Overall rating: **9 out of 10 perfectly preserved spice containers**.
