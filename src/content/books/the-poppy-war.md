## The Poppy War Review

### A Book About War (And Opium)

The novel opens with a war orphan who takes an exam to enter military school. She discovers she's a shaman.

Rin is brilliant and angry. She goes to war. The war is brutal.

Shamans can channel gods. This has consequences.

---

### Plot, Shamanically Speaking

The narrative follows Rin as she goes to war:

- She takes the Keju exam (surprisingly well)
- She attends Sinegard (military academy)
- She discovers her shamanic powers
- War with the Federation begins (brutal)
- She makes difficult choices (very difficult)

A poppy war is fought. It involves opium and fire.

---

### Characters, War-Touched

The cast is varied:

- **Rin** – Shaman. Powerful. Very angry
- **Jiang** – Teacher. Wise. Has secrets
- **Venka** – Classmate. Talented. Ambitious
- **Altan** – Shaman. Powerful. Damaged
- **The Federation** – Enemy. Brutal. Well-organized

Character development occurs through war and trauma.

---

### Themes, Shamanically

The book explores:

- the cost of war and revenge
- whether power is worth the price
- the nature of addiction (opium and otherwise)
- the importance of history

A description of war lasts several pages. It is somehow still interesting.

---

### Favourite Passage

> "War is not a game. It is a series of choices, each worse than the last."

This is repeated until it becomes a warning.

---

### Final Thoughts

This is a book about war, shamans, and difficult choices. It contains all three in equal measure.

Recommended for readers who enjoy:

- dark fantasy with historical influences
- powerful but damaged protagonists
- the cost of war and revenge
- shamanic magic systems

Overall rating: **8 out of 10 perfectly channeled gods**.
